Delete Account

Last updated: October 2026

You can permanently delete your Cres account directly within the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Cres
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Open Edit Profile
  4. Under Account Actions, tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm your identity when prompted
  6. Confirm permanent deletion

You may also open Settings → Terms & policy → Delete Account Information for this page.

Before deleting your account, Cres may ask you to verify your identity by re-authenticating with your linked sign-in method (password, Google, and/or Apple Sign In, as applicable).

If you signed in using Apple Sign In, Google Sign In, or Email & Password, deleting your Cres account does not delete your Apple account, Google account, or email account.

Only your Cres account and associated Cres data are affected.

Cres Plus and billing

Deleting your Cres account does not cancel an active subscription.

If you have Cres Plus through Apple In-App Purchase, cancel your subscription separately in Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. If you subscribed through Google Play on another device, cancel in Google Play subscription settings.

Otherwise, billing may continue until you cancel with Apple or Google.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes access to Cres and initiates removal of associated data, including:

  • Notes
  • Hidden Notes
  • Checklists
  • Hidden Checklists
  • Time Capsule sealed notes and checklists
  • Tasks
  • Habits
  • Mood entries
  • Vision Boards
  • Photos and attachments stored in your account
  • Settings and preferences

When your account is deleted, backend systems begin removing your cloud data. This process is intended to start immediately and may take additional time to complete depending on system load.

Account deletion is intended to be permanent and may not be reversible.
